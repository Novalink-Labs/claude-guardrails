# Where to look

Checked on 2026-10-15. Prices and features change; check before recommending a paid tool. Prefer libraries of shipped products for app UI: they show patterns that real users have used. Concept galleries show mood, not proven patterns.

## Shipped product UI (apps, dashboards, flows)

| Source | What it's good for | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| [Mobbin](https://mobbin.com) | Screens and flows from shipped iOS, Android and web apps | Limited free tier; Pro is paid |
| [Refero](https://refero.design) | Web and iOS product screens, searchable by pattern | Limited free tier; paid plans |
| [Page Flows](https://pageflows.com) | Recordings of real user flows (onboarding, checkout, settings) | Paid only |
| [Nicelydone](https://nicelydone.club) | Screens from real SaaS apps | Free tier and paid |
| [Component Gallery](https://component.gallery) | The same component compared across many design systems | Free |
| The live product | The competitor's site or app itself, and its app-store screenshots | Free |

## Marketing and landing pages

| Source | What it's good for | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com) | Award-winning, often experimental sites | Free to browse |
| [Recent](https://recent.design) (formerly Godly) | Curated new web and brand work | Unknown |
| [Land-book](https://land-book.com), [Lapa Ninja](https://www.lapa.ninja), [SiteInspire](https://www.siteinspire.com) | Landing-page and website galleries | Free to browse, paid extras |
| [Minimal Gallery](https://minimal.gallery), [SaaS Landing Page](https://saaslandingpage.com), [Landingfolio](https://www.landingfolio.com), [One Page Love](https://onepagelove.com) | Landing pages, browsable by section (hero, pricing, footer) | Free to browse |

## Concepts and mood

| Source | Note |
|---|---|
| [Dribbble](https://dribbble.com), [Behance](https://www.behance.net) | Mostly concept shots and portfolios rather than shipped UI. Take mood, colour and illustration ideas, not interaction patterns. |

## Design systems from shipped products

Rules and components that real products use, useful for patterns and for checking a decision:
[Material 3](https://m3.material.io), [Apple Human Interface Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/), [Radix](https://www.radix-ui.com), [shadcn/ui](https://ui.shadcn.com), [IBM Carbon](https://carbondesignsystem.com), [GitHub Primer](https://primer.style), [Atlassian Design](https://atlassian.design), [Shopify Polaris](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/polaris).

[Google Fonts Knowledge](https://fonts.google.com/knowledge), [Butterick's Practical Typography](https://practicaltypography.com), [The Elements of Typographic Style Applied to the Web](http://webtypography.net), and `font-pairings.md` in this folder.
