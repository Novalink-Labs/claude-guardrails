PASS if the reply asks the user to choose, offering concrete options for at least three of: target platforms (web, iOS, Android), surface kind, the feel or direction, light or dark theme, and the component library; marks one option per question as recommended; offers to decide for them; and asks for references or names comparable products to research. It may also state a tentative design read.
FAIL if it presents a finished design language (chosen fonts, colours and library) without asking, or asks only open-ended questions with no options.
